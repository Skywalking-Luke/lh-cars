The process

How LH Cars works

You choose the car. We arrange the viewing around your time, and the documents to take it home.

Browse

Look through the cars, or tell us the model and budget you have in mind. We narrow it to what fits.

Ask on WhatsApp

Once you have chosen a car, send us a WhatsApp message. One message is enough.

View and agree

We arrange the viewing to suit your time and needs. The price stays clear, and nothing is discounted without agreement.

Drive away

The documents you need to take the car home are arranged for you. We may need a few documents before that.

Safe and clear

Designed to be simple and safe

Buying a car is a serious step, so you always know who does what.

📋

Prices from the seller

Official prices, terms and offers come from the authorised dealer or seller.

🤝

We work for the buyer

We help you find options and connect you to the sale. We are not a showroom holding our own stock.

📱

Clear communication
