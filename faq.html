Buyer FAQ

Short answers about prices, viewing, documents and what LH Cars does. Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp.

Are prices shown on the listings?

Yes. Each listing shows the seller’s price, for example the 1964 MG Midget at Cash RM99,000, or the asking price on incoming stock. Ask on WhatsApp for current prices.

Can LH Cars give me a discount?

No. LH Cars does not set, change or discount selling prices. Any price, offer or terms are discussed and decided with the authorised seller.

Are the cars on the website real stock?

Yes. Cards marked Live listing or Incoming stock are real cars with real photos. Availability, exact specs and condition are confirmed when you enquire.

How do I arrange a viewing?

Send us a WhatsApp message. Once you have chosen a car, the viewing is arranged with the seller, and we tell you the time, place and what to bring.

Is financing available?

Financing, where available, is usually arranged through the dealer or their partner banks. Loan approval, rates and terms are decided by the lender, not by LH Cars. Ask us and we will point you in the right direction.

What documents do I usually need?

It depends on the deal (cash or loan, individual or company). Usually you need your IC, contact details and any loan documents. The seller will give you the exact list during the process.

What exactly does LH Cars do?

LH Cars is a broker that helps buyers: we find options, handle your enquiry and connect you to a proper sale with an authorised dealer or seller. The final price, negotiation, viewing, paperwork and completion go through the authorised seller.

How fast do you reply on WhatsApp?

We aim to reply as quickly as we can during normal hours. Tell us the model or what you need so we can give you a useful answer. Our number is +60128744878.

Where is LH Cars based?

LH Cars works as a WhatsApp-first broker for buyers across Malaysia. Viewing and handover depend on where the car is. The 1964 MG Midget is in Petaling Jaya (PJ).
